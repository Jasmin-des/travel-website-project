Experience a journey of stunning contrasts, from the glittering skylines of modern Singapore to the ancient temples and wild jungles of the Indonesian archipelago. Aboard the Heritage Adventure — chartered exclusively for our Road Scholar community — you’ll sail into a world of dramatic volcanoes, lush rainforests and spiritual wonders. With our experts, explore the serene majesty of Borobudur, the world’s largest Buddhist temple, and the jungles of Borneo, come face to face with gentle orangutans at the world-renowned Camp Leakey and in a remote Lombok village.

Best of all, you’ll...

  • Enjoy exclusive access to experts who will be with you throughout your journey and learn from them as you take in onboard lectures and educational activities.
  • Observe orangutans in their natural habitat during an unforgettable visit to Camp Leakey, the world-famous orangutan research and rehabilitation center in Borneo.
  • Discover the spiritual heart of Java as you explore the magnificent Borobudur, a 9th-century masterpiece of Buddhist architecture, and learn the stories carved into its volcanic stone.
  • Experience the vibrant culture of the archipelago firsthand, from a deep-dive exploration of Singapore to a visit with the weavers of Sukarara village on the island of Lombok.
